What this can and cannot tell you

  • It reports suspicious evidence for you to check. It does not prove an attack and the score is a review priority, not a probability.
  • A copied radio address with the same security and a believable signal can look like the real thing to a metadata-only check.
  • One adapter sees only what is in range, and signal moves with people and walls.
  • It reads access-point names and radio details. It never captures traffic, collects credentials or disconnects anyone.
